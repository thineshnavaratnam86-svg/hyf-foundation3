Cute Garden

Cats are small, intelligent mammals that are commonly kept as pets. They are independent but can also be very affectionate with people.

Cats mainly eat meat because they are carnivores. They communicate through meowing, purring, hissing, body movements, and facial expressions. Cats usually sleep around 12–16 hours a day. Cats clean themselves by licking their fur.

Persian

Friendly and fluffy cats with long, beautiful fur.

Siamese

Social and intelligent cats with distinctive coloring.

Maine Coon

Large, friendly cats known for their long fur.

Bengal

Active cats with beautiful spotted or marbled coats.

British Shorthair

Calm and affectionate cats with a thick short coat.
