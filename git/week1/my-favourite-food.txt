Chicken Fried Rice Recipe

Ingredients:
- Rice
- Chicken
- Eggs
- Carrots
- Peas
- Soy sauce
- Salt
- Pepper
- Cooking oil

Instructions:
1. Cook the rice and let it cool.
2. Cook the chicken in a pan.
3. Add carrots and peas.
4. Add the eggs and mix well.
5. Add the cooked rice.
6. Add soy sauce, salt, and pepper.
7. Stir-fry everything together and serve.
