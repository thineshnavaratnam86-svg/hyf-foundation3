Chicken Curry Recipe

Ingredients:
- Chicken
- Onion
- Garlic
- Ginger
- Curry powder
- Tomato
- Coconut milk
- Salt
- Cooking oil

Instructions:
1. Heat oil in a pan.
2. Fry the onion, garlic, and ginger.
3. Add curry powder and mix.
4. Add the chicken and cook until lightly browned.
5. Add tomato and coconut milk.
6. Add salt and simmer until the chicken is fully cooked.
7. Serve with rice.
